03 · The training in six courses

What each course adds to your loop. This one is the last.

1. Baseline

The five pieces of the loop (goal, state, action, verification, stop), the harness, today's loop engines, LOOP-R and the done criterion from 0 to 4.

2. Verification

Whoever says it's done can't be whoever did it: skeptical evaluator, frozen tests, shortcuts, gold set, calibrated judge and the layered gate.

3. Long-running execution

The seven-file kit, goal.md level 3+, context in bands, handoff that checks, headless loop with a ceiling and session measurement.

4. Multi-agent

When not to use it, subagents with short returns, generator × evaluator, worktrees, queues without collisions and the Claude + Codex team.

5. Security

Lethal trifecta, Rule of Two, injection through read content, permissions per step, guard hooks, sandbox and the door to the irreversible.

6. The loop that learns

Hypothesis with a number, keep/discard, promotion and rollback, lessons that become a binding rule, protected meter and cost per cycle.

E23 · Your loop, checked

2 h

In your project, copy loop.conf.exemplo to loop.conf, point each path to the pieces you built in the practice sections of lessons 1 to 7, and run conferir-loop.py. Close the missing pieces one at a time, in order.

Done when: python3 $K/conferir-loop.py ~/projetos/[your-project]; echo "saída: $?" → LOOP: 8/8 — PRONTO and saída: 0.

E24 · The first real cycle

1 h

With the loop at 8/8, run a real cycle with your agent, with the guardrail and hidden cases on and the trace recording (MEDIR and MEDIDOR exported as in lesson 2, if the measurer is your own). If it ends in keep, make the card and ask the person who decides for approval.

Done when: your system's results.tsv gets a new line, the trace gets a line with tokens, and there is a decisao.md with a name and date if the cycle ended in keep.
