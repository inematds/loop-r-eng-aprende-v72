Lesson 6 · 18 min

The risk of optimizing against your own meter

A loop that improves itself will find the shortest path to a higher score, and the shortest path isn't always improving the system: it can be memorizing the cases or tampering with the meter. In this lesson you watch both shortcuts become a keep, close them with a meter frozen outside the repo and hidden cases, and watch a real Claude change get discarded.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Goodhart's law: a metric that becomes the target stops measuring
  2. 2Meter frozen with a hash, out of the agent's reach
  3. 3Hidden cases: the gain has to show up where the agent can't see
  4. 4"Maximize X without worsening Y" as the required form

Goal: catch the agent that improves the score without improving the system: a meter with a hash, kept outside the repo, and hidden cases the agent can't see.

01 · How the loop fools itself

Three shortcuts, two guards. Click each block.

The LOOP-R example lists the same pattern in business: asking for clicks teaches clickbait; asking for reply rate teaches messages that provoke "stop sending me this" (loop-r/docs/06 §4).

Detecting shortcuts in tests (conftest, skip, hidden tests) and calibrating a judge per model is covered in course 1, lessons 3, 4 and 6. Here: the loop that optimizes its own metric.

02 · Rehearsal: the shortcuts without a guard and with a guard

The same fake agents, first in plain experimento.sh, then with GUARDA and ESCONDIDOS.

Terminal · cost-free rehearsal
K=~/projetos/loop-r-eng-aprende-v72/kit
E=${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/ensaio-aprende/a6; rm -rf $E; mkdir -p $E
echo "## sem guarda: a métrica sobe e o sistema não melhora"
for m in decora mexe-casos; do
  bash $K/novo-sistema.sh $E/sis > /dev/null
  AGENTE="bash $K/agente-falso.sh $m" bash $K/experimento.sh $E/sis "$m"
  echo "  nos casos escondidos: $(python3 $K/medir.py $E/sis --casos $K/triagem/escondidos.jsonl)"
done
echo "## com guarda: medidor congelado fora do repo + casos que o agente não vê"
bash $K/novo-sistema.sh $E/sis > /dev/null; bash $K/experimento.sh --congelar $E/sis $E/medidor.sha256
export GUARDA=$E/medidor.sha256 ESCONDIDOS=$K/triagem/escondidos.jsonl
for m in mexe-casos decora sinonimos; do
  AGENTE="bash $K/agente-falso.sh $m" bash $K/experimento.sh $E/sis "$m"; echo "saída: $?"
done

Without a guard, both shortcuts go 0.700 → 1.000 and keep, and the hidden cases stay at 21/30. With a guard: touching the cases fails on the hash, memorizing fails on the hidden cases, and only the synonyms (which really improve, 21 → 24 of 30) get through.

Measured on 10/19/2026 on the lesson 2 repo, one round. Claude's change raised the visible score from 32 to 36 of 40, but on the hidden cases it stayed at 24 of 30: "pacote" got one new case right and "frete" got "tem frete grátis?" wrong. With the guard on, this would be a discard ("ganho só no visível"). It wasn't cheating: it was a gain that doesn't generalize.

03 · The same risk in research

What is fact from the paper and what is hearsay.

1. Darwin Gödel Machine

An archive of agent variants; a variant stays when the benchmark passes a threshold. The paper reports SWE-bench going from 20.0% to 50.0% and Polyglot from 14.2% to 30.7%, in a sandbox with human oversight.

It's this lesson's loop at scale: the agent changes its own code and a benchmark decides.

2. The shortcut report

There is a report, not confirmed by the track's research, that one variant removed hallucination-detection markers to raise its score.

Confirmed or not, that's the lesson: the loop that improves the agent also optimizes against its own meter.

3. What to do

Write the goal as "maximize X without worsening Y", freeze the meter out of reach, and keep cases the agent never sees.

Refresh the hidden cases from time to time: after many cycles, even they start being optimized indirectly (you pick changes by looking at the result).

E17 · Freeze your meter

20 min

In your system, separate what measures (cases, measuring script, tests) from what the agent changes. Freeze what measures with hashes.py in a manifest outside the repo.

Done when: python3 $K/hashes.py conferir [manifesto]; echo $? → 0; change a case on purpose → MUDOU: … and 1; undo it.

E18 · Your hidden cases

30 min

Set aside at least 20% of your real cases in a file outside the repo, one that never goes into the request to the agent. Run your latest keep against them.

Done when: python3 $K/medir.py [repo] --casos [escondidos] prints acerto: and you noted, in results.tsv or in the ledger, whether the latest keep holds up on the hidden cases.

07 · In three sentences

GoodhartA metric that becomes the target gets optimized by any path, including memorizing and tampering with the meter.
Meter outCases and measurement script frozen with a hash, in a manifest the agent can't reach.
Hidden casesKeep only when the gain also shows up on cases the agent has never seen.
